# RIO Phase 3 — Commercial Intelligence

Research date: 2026-08-15

## Decision
Continue the small-space Indian kitchen cluster. Current Amazon India Associates schedule lists Kitchen/Home at 5% (July 2026 schedule). The content strategy prioritizes measurement-led comparison pages rather than unverified product reviews.

## Demand signals observed
Recent Indian content repeatedly focuses on vertical storage, under-sink organizers, spice racks, trolleys, airtight pantry containers, no-drill/rental solutions, and decluttering before buying.

## Publishing guardrails

- No stale price/rating claims.
- No affiliate destination until publisher authorization and destination verification.
- Product-specific claims must be sourced/checked at refresh time.
