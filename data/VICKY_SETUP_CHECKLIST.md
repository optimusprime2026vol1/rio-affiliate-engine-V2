Drafted 2026-08-16 by Dr. Victor, per BUSINESS_STRATEGY.md "Immediate next
actions" item 2. These are the founder-only actions blocking RIO's next
phase -- each one is a one-time, roughly 5-15 minute task. RIO cannot do any
of these (account creation and credential handling are founder-only, Section
6). Do them whenever you have a few minutes; none are urgent same-day.

## 1. EarnKaro signup (adds Flipkart, Myntra, Ajio and 150+ merchants)

- Go to earnkaro.com and sign up (free, no documents beyond basic KYC).
- Once approved, note your EarnKaro affiliate ID/dashboard access.
- Tell me (or drop the confirmation screenshot) once it's live -- I'll wire
  it into the pipeline the same way Amazon Associates is wired in now.
- Why: 30-day cookie (vs Amazon's 24-hour) and covers merchants Amazon
  doesn't, per BUSINESS_STRATEGY.md's diversification rule (no single
  merchant above 30% of revenue by month 12).

## 2. Telegram channel creation

- Create a Telegram channel (public, so it's discoverable/shareable).
- Suggested name pattern: something short and practical, e.g. "Space-Smart
  Home India Deals" -- your call on the exact name/handle.
- Add a one-line channel description mentioning it contains affiliate links.
- Once created, share the channel @handle/link with me -- I already have a
  ready-to-post content format and a rotating list of 10 verified offers
  waiting (see `TELEGRAM_DEAL_DROP_FORMAT.md`).

## 3. AdSense signup (pure pageview revenue, no sale needed)

- Go to google.com/adsense and sign up with the site's domain
  (vickykenin-lang.github.io/rio-affiliate-engine, or a custom domain if we
  get one later -- either works for signup).
- No minimum traffic required to apply; approval can take a few days.
- Once approved, share the AdSense publisher ID -- I'll add the required ad
  slots to the site template. This stacks on top of affiliate revenue
  without needing a sale to happen (per the case-study data in
  BUSINESS_STRATEGY.md's broader-scale-levers section).
