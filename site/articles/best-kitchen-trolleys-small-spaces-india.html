A slim trolley can convert narrow gaps into movable storage. Width, wheel quality, load stability and cleaning access matter more than the number of tiers.

What to check before buying

  • Measure the narrowest point of the intended gap.
  • Check wheel clearance and whether the trolley can be pulled out fully.
  • Store heavier items on lower tiers for stability.
